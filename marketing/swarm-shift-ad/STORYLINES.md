# SWARM//SHIFT social ad: storylines

The brief: a vertical ad for TikTok, Instagram Reels and Shorts, built from real gameplay, to run once the game is on the App Store. The person asked for no stops, so A was built; B and C are kept here so a later turn can switch.

## A. One Run (built, recommended)

The run's own clock is the spine: a stranger follows a timer from 0:00 to the boss at 5:00 without reading a word.

- **Device / form:** the result first, then a number that lands (the run clock). Cut on the beat, with the brand's "//" as the wipe.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** a lone core, then the camera pulls back on a ring of 400+ enemies closing in. Rewind to 0:00: one thumb, one gun. Pick 1 of 3. The clock leaps through minutes two and three as a different weapon tears through each arena colour; by minute four it is absurd; at 5:00 the Overseer arrives and falls. Logo, play.
- **First two seconds:** frame 0 is the lone cyan core with its orbit blades and "ONE CORE." already on screen; at 0.5 s the camera pulls back and "ENDLESS SWARM." lands as the ring fills the frame.
- **Beats:** hook (core, ring) → 0:00 drag to move → pick 1 of 3 → 2:08 Seeker Missiles, 2:30 Prism Lance, 3:08 Singularity, 3:34 Plasma Mines → 4:25 ABSURD → 4:55 warning, 5:00 the Overseer, the kill → end card (icon, SWARM//SHIFT, PLAY FREE).
- **Look:** the game's own: navy #05070D, Open Sans caps tracked like the in-game banners, cyan #2DE2FF for the player, magenta #FF3D9A rationed to the "//".
- **Sound:** the game's 124 BPM combat track from bar 5, so the call to action lands on the A minor downbeat; the game's own sound effects from each take; the music muffles during the level-up pick exactly as the game does, and opens on the tap.

## B. Which would you pick?

- **Device / form:** a question with the input; split screen to one.
- **What the viewer sees:** the real level-up screen asks "CHOOSE AN UPGRADE"; the ad asks "WHICH ONE?". Each of the three cards plays its weapon against the same swarm in three stacked panes, then the panes collapse into the one that wins.
- **First two seconds:** the three cards on screen, a thumb hovering over them.
- **Sound:** the card sound, then each weapon's own sound in turn.

## C. One thumb

- **Device / form:** a character does the work (a real thumb); one continuous take.
- **What the viewer sees:** a real thumb on a real iPhone drags, and the line it draws becomes the core's path through the swarm, unbroken from 0:00 to the boss.
- **Needs:** hands filmed on a phone, which cannot be captured from Godot.
